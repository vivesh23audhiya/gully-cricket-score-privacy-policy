Privacy Policy

Gully My Cricket Score
Effective date: 13 September 2026

This policy explains how Gully My Cricket Score (“the App”) handles information when you use the Android app (com.gully_my_cricket_score).

1. What the App does

The App lets you create teams and players, score live cricket matches, view scorecards, and optionally back up your local database to Google Drive.

2. Information we store on your device

Most data stays on your phone, including:

  • Match, innings, player, and team records
  • Optional profile photos for players and teams
  • App preferences saved on the device

We do not operate a public account system. You can use scoring without signing in.

3. Photos and camera

If you add a player or team photo, the App may use the camera or photo library. Images are stored on your device and are included only if you create a backup. We do not upload photos to our own servers.

4. Google Sign-In and Drive backup

Backup is optional. If you choose “Sign in with Google”, the App asks Google for access to create and manage only files the App creates on your Drive (backup files). We receive your Google account name and email so we can show which account is signed in.

Backup files may contain your match, team, player, and photo data. They are stored in your Google Drive. You can delete backups from the App or Drive, and you can sign out at any time.

5. Notifications and Firebase

The App uses Google Firebase for:

  • Cloud Messaging — a device token so we can send app alerts
  • Remote Config — store links and minimum version for updates

Firebase may collect standard device and app diagnostics as described in Google’s Firebase privacy information.

6. Other permissions

  • Internet — Google Sign-In, Drive backup, Firebase, and opening store links
  • Notifications — match or app alerts (you can disable these in system settings)

7. Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share match or player records with advertisers. If you use the share feature, you choose what to send through another app (for example WhatsApp).

Google processes Sign-In, Drive, and Firebase data under Google’s Privacy Policy.

8. Children

The App is a general cricket scoring tool and is not directed at children under 13. Do not submit a child’s personal information through the App.

9. Data retention and your choices

  • Uninstalling the App deletes local data on that device.
  • You can delete Drive backups you created.
  • You can revoke Google access in your Google Account permissions.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
